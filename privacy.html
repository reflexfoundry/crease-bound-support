Privacy Policy

Crease Bound · Last updated 13 September 2026

Crease Bound does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and no network requests of any kind.

Information collected

None. The app does not ask for, gather, or receive any personal information. It requests no permissions — no access to your contacts, photos, location, camera, microphone, or health data.

Information stored on your device

The only information the app saves is your own game progress, kept locally on your device:

  • which levels you have completed and how far you have unlocked,
  • your sound and haptics preferences.

This never leaves your device. It is not transmitted anywhere, and the developer has no access to it. Deleting the app removes it permanently.

Network use

Crease Bound makes no network connections. Every level is contained in the app itself, and the game is fully playable offline. There is no server component.

Third parties

The app contains no third-party software development kits, advertising networks, analytics services, crash reporters, or social media integrations. No third party receives any information about you through this app.

Tracking

The app does not track you. It does not use the Advertising Identifier (IDFA), does not build a profile of you, and does not link any data to your identity across apps or websites owned by other companies.

Children's privacy

Crease Bound is suitable for all ages and is rated 4+. Because the app collects no data whatsoever, it collects no data from children. It complies with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) by collecting nothing.

Your rights

Regulations such as the GDPR and CCPA grant rights to access, correct, or delete personal data held about you. No such data exists here — nothing is collected or stored outside your own device — so there is nothing to request, export, or erase. Deleting the app removes the locally stored progress.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date. Any future change that introduced data collection would also require an app update and a new App Store privacy declaration.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to qblink.techno@gmail.com.
